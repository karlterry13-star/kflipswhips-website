Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Karl's Signature Motors, also known as KFlipsWhips ("we," "us," or "our"), is a personal car-shopping and auto brokerage service based in South Florida. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit kflipswhips.com, fill out one of our forms (including forms on Facebook and Instagram), or contact us, and how we use and protect it.

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you submit a form, message us, or call or text us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address, city, and county
  • Vehicle details, such as the make, model, or type you're interested in, your budget, your timeline, and whether you have a trade-in
  • Optional buying details you choose to share to help us plan your purchase, such as an estimated credit range, prior financing history, or whether you'll have a co-applicant
  • Anything else you include in your messages to us

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our site and hosting providers may automatically collect basic technical information, such as your browser type, device, IP address, pages visited, and referring site. Advertising platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) may use cookies or similar technologies to measure how our ads perform.

Information from advertising platforms. If you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares the information you entered on that form with us.

How We Use Your Information

  • Contact you by phone, text, or email about your car search
  • Find vehicles that match what you're looking for and compare options for you
  • Coordinate the buying process, including trade-ins, with dealerships on your behalf
  • Send you updates, vehicle options, and occasional offers related to your search
  • Improve our website, services, and advertising
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it as needed to help you buy a vehicle or to run our business:

  • Dealerships and sellers. We share relevant details with the dealership or seller involved in your purchase so we can locate, price, and coordinate your vehicle.
  • Financing partners. If you choose to apply for financing, your information goes to the dealership or lender handling that application. We only do this after you ask us to.
  • Service providers. These companies help us operate: website hosting, database, email, text-messaging, and advertising providers. They may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may share information if required by law, to respond to legal process, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Text Messages and Calls

When you give us your phone number, you agree that we may contact you by call or text about your vehicle search. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. We do not share your phone number or text-messaging opt-in with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of messages: Reply STOP to texts, use the unsubscribe link in emails, or tell us you no longer want to be contacted.
  • Access, correct, or delete your information: Email us at kflipswhips@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Ad preferences: You can manage how Meta uses your data for ads in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

Data Retention and Security

We keep your information only as long as needed to help with your car search, maintain our business records, and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a new effective date.
